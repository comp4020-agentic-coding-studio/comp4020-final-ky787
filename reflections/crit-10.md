# Crit 9 reflection


## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I played the 5 draft levels with a friend to see what needed changing and found that assets with generic looking designs like the levels, buttons and cubes were confusing and not intuiative. To rectify this I got chatgpt/claude to iterate through various designs to get more intuitive assets designs. For example, the first iteration produced a cube with a software themed asset and the socket to put the cubes was a large industrial looking thing to accept it. However after re-prompting I got a new design which had a cube and socket that were animated where the cube would snap into place with the socket and both would pulsate green which indicates the cube belongs there. Overall the new assets proved a much more intuitive design. 

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

When designing the game with agents it is easy to see unintuiative designs as easy to use especially when getting positive feedback from AI agents, which highlights the importance of getting human input for reviews.
