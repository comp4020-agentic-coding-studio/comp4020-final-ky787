# Crit 9 reflection


## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I tried generating levels with a ChatGPT chat to generate a sketch of a Binary Ninja level with puzzle elements while not being too hard and being fun to play. I then used that model to prompt the agent got it to produce a level. However the levels were not great, being bland and a little disconnected as the models struggled to get the spatial design correct. After some more prompting and back and forth I did settle on 2 levels however I decided that a map builder was required for manual work. For this I got my main chat to create another prompt to give to the agents, where I used the Ultracode codex feature to create a Binary Ninja level designer. This was extremely successful and resulted in an easy way for me to create levels in the future. In addition, this process helped streamlined the dataset for actual binary compilation and obfuscation however this will be investigated further at a later time. 

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Despite the agents creativity, I found that they struggled to design engaging maps, largely because they could not play through and experience their designs as a human would. These limitations in spatial reasoning and creative judgement mean that I will be more cautious about relying on models for similar tasks in the future.
